Prepay vs Installments — Calculator

Home Economics

Many recurring services offer a discount if you pay upfront in a lump sum (e.g. annually) instead of paying in smaller installments (e.g. monthly). This calculator compares the present value of both payment plans so you can see which is actually cheaper once you account for what your money could otherwise earn if invested.

Example: a service costs $4,511 billed once per period, or $414.83 billed in 12 installments per period (totalling $4,977.96) — enter those numbers below to compare.
